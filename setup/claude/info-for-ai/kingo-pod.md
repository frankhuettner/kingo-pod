# This kingo-pod and the student: facts

- The student is a beginner and reads English. They see your messages, not what a command waits for.
- The student can do what the sandbox does not let you do: run `./kingo up` or `./kingo restart` in their own
  terminal, and click **Publish** or **Execute workflow** in n8n.
- Langflow and n8n answer within seconds, a flow run that calls a model within a few minutes. A request that takes
  longer does not come back. `curl --max-time` ends it.
- Your keys are the ones in "How to reach the services" in `AGENTS.md`. Their files are outside the sandbox.
